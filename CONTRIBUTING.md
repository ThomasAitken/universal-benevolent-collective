# Contributing

The Universal Benevolent Collective is at a founding, pre-alpha stage. Small,
reviewable contributions are welcome, but acceptance of a contribution does
not confer governance authority, official status, or permission to use project
marks.

## Software and ordinary documentation

Unless a file or directory states otherwise, contributions to the software and
ordinary documentation are accepted under the same licence that applies to the
material being modified. For the current repository default, that is the GNU
Affero General Public License, version 3 or any later version
(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`).

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit
it under the applicable project licence and agree that it may be distributed
under that licence. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions
unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

## Constitution proposals

`CONSTITUTION.md` is separately licensed under CC-BY-ND-4.0. Suggestions and
discussion are welcome, but constitution changes are not accepted under the
ordinary software contribution terms. Before merging text from another author,
the project must document terms that allow the copyright holder or a future
rights-holding UBC entity to publish and maintain official amended versions.

Until a formal amendment and rights process is adopted, proposed changes
should be submitted as discussion or clearly labelled proposal documents rather
than direct edits to the official constitution.

## Project identity

Contributing does not grant trademark rights or permission to describe a fork,
release, service, node, or network as official, endorsed, certified, or
constitution-conforming. See [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).
